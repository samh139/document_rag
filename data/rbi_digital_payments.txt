RESERVE BANK OF INDIA – DIGITAL PAYMENTS GUIDELINES

1. UPI Guidelines
- Real-time transfers.
- No charges for P2P transfers.
- Limits vary by bank and app.

2. NEFT
- 24×7 availability.
- Settlement in half-hourly batches.

3. RTGS
- Real-time gross settlement.
- High-value transactions only.

4. IMPS
- Real-time, 24×7.
- Limits vary by bank.

5. Card Payments
- Tokenization recommended.
- OTP required for online transactions.

6. Wallets
- Full KYC required for higher limits.
- Interoperability allowed.
