STATE BANK OF INDIA – LOAN PRODUCTS

1. Home Loan
- Available for purchase, construction, or renovation.
- Attractive interest rates.
- Tenure up to 30 years.
- Processing fees applicable.
- No prepayment penalty on floating rate loans.

2. Personal Loan (Xpress Credit)
- Available to salaried individuals.
- Quick disbursal.
- Minimum documentation.
- EMI-based repayment.

3. Auto Loan
- For purchase of new or pre-owned cars.
- Tenure up to 7 years.
- Competitive interest rates.

4. Education Loan
- Covers tuition fees, hostel charges, etc.
- Moratorium period available.
- Interest subsidy available for eligible students.

5. MSME Loans
- Cash credit and term loans.
- CGTMSE coverage available for eligible borrowers.
- Working capital finance.

6. Gold Loan
- Secured against gold ornaments.
- Quick processing.
- Overdraft and term loan variants.

7. Loan Against Property
- Loan based on property value.
- Tenure up to 15 years.
- For personal or business use.
