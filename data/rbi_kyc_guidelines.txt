RESERVE BANK OF INDIA – BASIC KYC GUIDELINES

1. KYC Documentation
- Proof of Identity (POI): Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, Voter ID.
- Proof of Address (POA): Aadhaar, Utility bill, Bank statement.
- For minors: identity of guardian required.
- For NRIs: passport and overseas address proof.

2. Simplified KYC (Small Accounts)
- Low-risk accounts.
- Restrictions on balance and total credits.
- Valid for 12 months, extendable to 24 months.

3. Periodic KYC Updates
- Based on risk category: low/moderate/high.
- Banks must notify customers before freezing accounts.

4. Aadhaar-based e-KYC
- Allowed for banks with UIDAI authentication permission.

5. Video KYC
- Customer verification through video-based process.
