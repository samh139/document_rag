STATE BANK OF INDIA – ACCOUNT PRODUCTS

1. Savings Account (Regular)
- Suitable for individuals
- Minimum balance requirement depends on region
- Free RuPay Debit Card
- Passbook available
- SMS alert facility

2. Basic Savings Bank Deposit (BSBD) Account
- Zero balance account
- No minimum balance required
- Free ATM-cum-Debit card
- Designed for financial inclusion
- Maximum 4 free withdrawals per month (branch/ATM)

3. Current Account
- For businesses and firms
- Higher free transaction limits
- Cheque book facility
- No interest paid on current account balances

4. Fixed Deposits (FD)
- Tenure: 7 days to 10 years
- Higher interest rates for senior citizens
- Premature withdrawal allowed with penalty
- Loans can be taken against FD

5. Recurring Deposits (RD)
- Monthly installment-based deposit
- Tenure 1–10 years
- Premature withdrawal allowed with penalty

6. NRI Accounts
- NRE: fully repatriable
- NRO: for income earned in India
- FCNR(B): in foreign currency
