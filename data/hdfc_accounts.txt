HDFC BANK – ACCOUNT PRODUCTS

1. Savings Account
- Minimum balance varies by account variant.
- Free debit card depending on type.
- NetBanking and MobileBanking included.

2. Current Account
- For businesses.
- Cash transactions and free limits vary by plan.
- Overdraft available for eligible customers.

3. Fixed Deposits (FD)
- Flexible tenure.
- Higher interest for seniors.
- Premature withdrawal penalty.

4. Salary Account
- Zero balance facility.
- Corporate tie-ups required.
- Special offers on loans and credit cards.

5. NRI Accounts
- NRE, NRO, and FCNR deposits.
