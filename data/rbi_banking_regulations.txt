RESERVE BANK OF INDIA – BASIC BANKING REGULATIONS

1. Customer Rights
- Right to fair treatment.
- Right to transparency in interest rates and charges.
- Right to suitability in products offered.

2. Fair Practice Code
- Banks must disclose all charges.
- No mis-selling.
- Written communication for loan rejection.

3. Depositor Protection
- DICGC insurance covers Rs. 5 lakh per customer per bank.

4. Loan Regulations
- Repo rate influences lending rates.
- KYC mandatory for all borrowers.
- EMI transparency required.

5. Grievance Redressal
- Banks must have internal ombudsman.
- RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme (RB-IOS) applies.
